Common questions

Is FlatmateMatch free to use? +

Yes — creating a profile and browsing matches is completely free for students. Premium features like priority placement are optional.

How is the compatibility score calculated? +

We weight budget overlap (30%), lifestyle habits (30%), sleep schedule (20%), and shared interests (20%) to produce your overall match score.

Which universities are supported? +

We support all UK universities. Just type your university name when signing up — if we don't recognise it, we'll add it within 24 hours.
